A calm money notebook,

Mockup v5 keeps the Paper & Ink design and trims how recaps are reached. Home now shows one quiet line on days 1–3, Profile has a single Recaps row, and a new Recaps page lets you pick any month or year that has transactions. PIN is an optional backup next to biometrics. The interface is in English, and the app is called Tally from one constant.

    02What's new in v5

    Colours, type and components are unchanged from v3 and v4.

    Home

    • The two recap cards are gone.
    • On days 1–3 of a month, one small line says "Your September recap is ready" and opens that recap. It stays hidden if last month has no transactions.
    • Layout now follows the spec: chips, balance, drafts banner, favourites, donut, trend, latest transactions, insights.
    • The budget ring and top-spend cards were removed (not in the Home spec). Period chips now include 1 Year and All.

    Recaps page

    • New screen with a year picker and a month grid, opened from one row in Profile.
    • Only periods up to the current month are listed. Nothing in the future.
    • Periods without transactions are greyed out and cannot be tapped. In the sample data: 2025, June and July.
    • The current month and year carry a "so far" label.

    Profile

    • The three-row Recaps list became a single "Recaps" row.
    • Security: Biometric lock, PIN lock (On or Off, "Optional backup") and Auto-lock.
    • Automation: Auto-detect, Drafts, Recurring.

    One dataset

    • Every recap is calculated from the same sample transactions, so cards agree with each other and with Home.
    • Jun and Jul have no transactions, so the yearly recap, Home "This year" and "3 Months" all use the same totals.
    • The priciest September day (24 Sep, Rp 476.000) matches the latest transactions list.

    Demo controls

    • "Simulate day 2" moves the mockup date to 2 Oct 2026 so the Home recap line appears and September becomes a full month.
    • "PIN" toggle shows how the unlock screen changes when no PIN is set.
    All numbers in this mockup are sample data, not real metrics. Currency uses Indonesian rupiah formatting (Rp 1.250.000).
